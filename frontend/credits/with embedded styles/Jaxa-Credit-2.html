AW3D30 Dataset & Engineering Terms

ALOS PRISM DSM

1. Engineering Fitness & Disclaimer

Data rendered is based on the ALOS Global Digital Surface Model (AW3D30). Pursuant to JAXA's Terms of Use, JAXA assumes no responsibility regarding the reliability of information or damages resulting from its use.

  • Surface Model vs. Bare Earth: Heights represent DSM elevations (including vegetation canopy and structures), not bare-earth terrain (DTM).
  • Application Limits: Designated for catchment-scale reconnaissance and macro-hydrology. Detailed bridge, culvert, and roadway designs require certified ground-truth topographic surveys (RTK-GNSS/Total Station/LiDAR).

2. Mandatory Source Attribution

In compliance with JAXA terms, all renders and derived analytics must indicate: "ALOS Global Digital Surface Model (AW3D30) © Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (JAXA)".
