Engineering Usage Notes

Design Restriction

This dataset is designated for preliminary reconnaissance, catchment-scale hydrology, and regional planning. It is a Digital Surface Model (DSM) that includes vegetation and structural canopies, not a bare-earth terrain model (DTM).

  • Not for Detailed Design: Satellite surface models do not substitute certified ground-truth topographic surveys or airborne LiDAR.
  • Geotechnical Verification: Regional elevations must be calibrated against official local benchmarks prior to making structural or finished grade decisions.
