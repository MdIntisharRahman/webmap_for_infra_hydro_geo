In accordance with JAXA's Terms of Use, the provider assumes no responsibility for data accuracy, completeness, or fitness for any particular purpose. This data is rendered strictly for preliminary reconnaissance, catchment-scale hydrology, and regional planning.

3. Structural & Civil Design Restriction

  • Not an Alternative to the Detailed Surveys: Satellite-derived surface models shall not substitute certified ground-truth topographic surveys (Total Station/RTK-GNSS) or airborne LiDAR.
  • Geotechnical & Hydraulic Verification: Regional elevations and hydro-meteorological estimates must be calibrated against certified local benchmarks and field-verified hydrographic records prior to structural footing, pier scour, or finished grade determination.
