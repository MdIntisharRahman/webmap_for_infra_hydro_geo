Engineering Usage Notes

Structural Scope & Planning Applicability

Data layers derived from consultancy master plans, feasibility reports, and design models represent baseline technical parameters for macro-level coordination and infrastructure research.

  • Corridor & Transportation Scoping: Applicable for preliminary route corridor screening, cross-sectoral infrastructure planning (Hatirjheel, Elevated Expressways, riverbank highways), and multi-agency coordination with RHD/LGED baselines.
  • Hydrologic & Water Resource Modeling: Provides contextual alignment for embankment protections, drainage structures, regulatory khal interventions, and flood control systems across active river deltas.
  • Site-Specific Design Mandate: Compilation datasets do not substitute for detailed, project-specific geotechnical borings (SPT/CPT), bathymetric sounding, or certified structural calculations required by the Bangladesh National Building Code (BNBC).
