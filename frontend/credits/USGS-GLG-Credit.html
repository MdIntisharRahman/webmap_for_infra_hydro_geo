Provenance & Attribution

The surface geology vector coverage served in this webmap originates from USGS Open-File Report 97-470-H (Geologic and Geophysical Data of Bangladesh)[cite: 2], compiled by the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) in collaboration with the Geological Survey of Bangladesh (GSB)[cite: 2].

Digitized from the 1990 Geological Map of Bangladesh (1:1,000,000) by Md. Khurshid Alam, A.K.M. Shahidul Hasan, Mujibur Rahman Khan (GSB), and John W. Whitney (USGS)[cite: 2]. Coastlines and international boundaries incorporate base references from ESRI ArcWorld 1:3M and NIMA VMAP0[cite: 2].

Alam, M.K., Hasan, A.K.M.S., Khan, M.R., and Whitney, J.W., 1990 (Digital compilation by USGS, 2001). Surface Geology of Bangladesh (b_geog / geo8bg), U.S. Geological Survey Open-File Report 97-470-H. https://doi.org/10.5066/P9HZQA15

Key Attribute Schema

Engineers and GIS analysts can filter geology using the core attributes:

  • GLG (Lithostratigraphy): Categorizes mapped formations such as csd (Beach sand), asd/asl/asc (Alluvial sand/silt/clay), rb (Barind clay residuum), rm (Madhupur clay residuum), QTdt (Dupi Tila Formation), Tt (Tipam Sandstone), Tb (Bhuban Formation), and Tba (Barail Formation).
  • TYPE (General Class): High-level classification including bed (bedrock), sed (sedimentary deposits), water (rivers/oceans), and lake (inland water bodies).

Engineering Usage Notes

Regional Scoping & Geotechnical Limitations

This layer represents regional surficial outcrops at 1:1,000,000 scale. Features under 200 meters in width or length were not captured during digitization.

  • Corridor Screening & Morphological Context: Recommended for preliminary highway alignment studies, regional soil provenance mapping (e.g., Madhupur/Barind tracts vs. active floodplains), and macro-scale borrow source appraisal.
  • Not for Foundation Design: This regional vector coverage does not replace site-specific geotechnical investigations (SPT, CPT, borehole logging, laboratory testing) mandated by highway and bridge engineering codes.
  • Data License: Boundary data includes ESRI intellectual property used under license for internal viewing and research.
