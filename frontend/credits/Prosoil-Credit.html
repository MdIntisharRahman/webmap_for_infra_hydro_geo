Engineering Usage Notes

Subsurface Variability & Planning Use

Borehole logs depict discrete localized stratigraphy, Standard Penetration Test (SPT-N) values, and groundwater levels measured at specific geographical coordinates and testing epochs[cite: 2].

  • Preliminary Alignment & Feasibility Studies: Highly valuable for regional soil profile interpolation, corridor preliminary foundation type scoping, identifying soft alluvium horizons, and estimating general pile termination depths across Bangladesh.
  • Spatial Interpolation Caveat: Riverine, deltaic, and active floodplain geomorphologies exhibit significant lateral stratigraphical variability. Regional logs must not substitute mandatory site-specific geotechnical investigations for final bridge substructure, abutment, or pier foundation design.
  • Geodetic Datum Context: Coordinates are mapped to WGS 84 (EPSG:4326) with depth logs standardized in meters below existing ground level (EGL).
